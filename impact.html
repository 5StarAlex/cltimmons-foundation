A Message from Our Founder

Our community showed up for one another.

Our first Cathy's Closet Pop-Up showed me how ready this community is to show up for one another. Twenty volunteers gave 160 hours to set up, sort, and serve, and together we welcomed 229 households. One guest stopped on his way out and told us, “Thank you! You just don't know how this helped and came at the right time.” That is why we do this work.

None of it happens without our partners. Thank you to SouthState Bank (Hoffmeyer Road Branch) and BHDD Pee Dee for sponsoring the event; to Gold Star Urgent Care, Tomlinson's Wholesale Warehouse, Draper G. Myers Mortuary, and Mount Rona Missionary Baptist Church for serving as drop-off sites; and to Mon-Dae Morning Child Care Center, The Jonathan Foundation, and Pee Dee Coalition for their donations and resources. Thank you as well to Tonya Brown and WPDE ABC 15 for helping us share the story. Read the WPDE coverage.

I know Cathy is happy and proud that her foundation is continuing to give back to this community and advocate for mental health awareness. We are carrying that forward into Cathy's Care Crates on December 11, where we hope to serve 250 to 300 community members.

Tatyana LanceFounder & President, Cathy Lance-Timmons Foundation
