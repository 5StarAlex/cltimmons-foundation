Sponsorship Packet

Partner with the foundation through a formal sponsorship.

Sponsorships help fund scholarships, community outreach, Cathy's Closet, educational programming, and special initiatives that carry Cathy Lance Timmons' legacy forward with care and dignity.

Download the sponsorship packet to review opportunities, complete it offline, and send the finished packet to partnerships@cltimmons.org. You can also submit the digital sponsorship form below, which sends the same information directly to the partnerships team.

Sponsorship Levels

Choose the partnership level that fits your commitment.

$5,000

Legacy Partner

Our highest level of partnership, for organizations prepared to anchor a full program year. Includes naming recognition on a program element and a speaking moment in the event program.

$2,500

Dignity Partner

Underwrites an entire Community Store pop up. Strong fit for regional employers, health systems, and financial institutions.

$1,000

Compassion Partner

Sustains a full donation drive cycle. A natural entry point for established local businesses and civic organizations.

$500

Hope Partner

Equips a single pop up event. Well suited to owner operated businesses, professional practices, and small firms.

$250

Community Friend

Stocks essentials for families in need. Designed for individuals, families, small shops, and community groups.

Varies

In-Kind Partner

Contributions of goods, services, printing, space, transportation, or volunteer hours in place of a financial gift. Welcome at any scale.

Custom

Custom Partnership

For organizations or families that want to set a different sponsorship amount or shape a custom partnership with the foundation.
